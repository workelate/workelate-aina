How we work

The pyramid was built for a world where software took armies.
That world ended.

A large SI, Accenture, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, or a mid-tier one like DXC or Coforge, quotes your build in phases, FTEs and quarters, because their economics need a pyramid of billable people. Your project is their revenue line; the pitch to you is a cheaper rate. Neither side is pricing the outcome. We change that equation: a fixed price tied to the build, two accountable partners, and an agent fleet doing the volume a floor of juniors used to bill for, delivering in weeks what the pyramid delivers in fiscal years.

Why 10× is arithmetic, not bravado

Where the speed actually comes from

10×

faster delivery. Diagnostic in 2 weeks, working system in weeks, not a phase-gate program that ships slideware for two quarters.

1/10

the team. Two accountable partners and agents, no pyramid of juniors learning on your invoice.

3–4×

lower cost. The savings from removing the pyramid go to you, not to our margin.

01

Agents do the volume, seniors do the judgment

Code generation, testing, documentation, data plumbing, report drafting, the 80% of delivery hours that juniors bill for, run on our agent fleet. Two or three senior people with agents now produce what 8–10 traditional engineers produce. The senior time goes where it matters: understanding your operation and making the calls.

02

We build one system at a time

No portfolio of 12 workstreams sharing a steering committee. One system, one accountable pair, weekly demos on your real data. Focus is the second-biggest speed multiplier after agents.

03

The verify loop: nobody grades their own homework

Every build runs inside a deterministic verification gate, automated checks that block "done" until the system provably works on your data. The same discipline applies to our agents in production: nothing counts as shipped until it provably works on your data. This is how a 2-person firm ships with enterprise-grade governance.

04

Fixed price, priced after diagnosis

The Diagnostic Sprint (2 weeks, fixed fee) produces the build plan with the price on it. We can price fixed because agents make our costs predictable, and because we've done the diagnosis, not estimated from a proposal template. If the Sprint finds nothing worth building, we tell you in writing.

05

The savings go to you

Removing the pyramid removes most of the cost. We keep senior-firm margins on a small team; you pay 3–4× less than an SI quote for the same outcome. For a $5–50M business that means a serious product build at $30K–$100K instead of a seven-figure program.

06

We run on what we sell

WE_AINA is WorkElate's own agency, our delivery runs on WorkElate, the AI-native operating system we build. Your engagement is scoped, built and run on the same platform we ship to clients. If our product could not run a real agency at commercial stakes, we would be the first to know, and the first to pay for it.
