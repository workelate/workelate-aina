What changed, operation by operation

RockProsUSA operations before and after the WE_AINA build
OperationBeforeAfter
Dispatch Phone and WhatsApp between 13 site managers; whoever shouted loudest got the truck. One queue across all sites; the Brain proposes the assignment with its reasoning, a dispatcher approves.

Why the numbers moved

Three mechanisms

01

The assignment carries its reasoning

Reasoning makes approval fast; the 38% lives in the minutes between an order existing and a truck confirmed.

02

The match happens on delivery, not at month end

Ticket, note and order reconcile as the haul completes, so 2,140 invoices went out with zero manual touches.

03

The report is a by-product, not a task

The report is assembled from records already written, by 6:00 AM; those hours are a large share of the 11,200 returned.
