Industrial ops, USA

RockProsUSA: 13 quarries on one dispatch system

Dispatch, invoicing and reporting moved onto one system across 13 quarry sites. The Brain proposes each truck assignment with its reasoning and a dispatcher approves it; invoices are matched against the delivery and the order without a manual pass; the daily report is written by 6 AM, ready for a person to read. Over the first twelve months: dispatch cycle time 38% lower, 11,200 operations hours returned.
