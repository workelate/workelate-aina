Industrial ops, USA

Southwest quarries: 13 sites on one dispatch system

Dispatch, invoicing and reporting moved onto one system across 13 quarry sites, with three native iPhone apps for customers, drivers and haulers. The Brain proposes each truck assignment with its reasoning and a dispatcher approves it; invoices are matched against the delivery and the order without a manual pass; the daily report is written by 6 AM. Over the first twelve months: 2,140 invoices with zero manual touches, 11,200 operations hours returned.
