The shape we look for first

Rankings earned. Clicks parked on page two.

Most clicks go to the top three results. A site can rank for a thousand keywords and still not grow, because its rankings sit at positions 4 to 20, where almost nobody clicks.

That is not solved by publishing more. It is a foundations problem, and the cheapest growth a mature site has.

Organic search symptoms, with the direct cause and the deeper cause behind each
What you seeDirect causeDeeper cause, the one that brings it back
Keywords stuck at positions 11 to 20The page answers the query only partly, and few internal links point at it.Trusted sites link to the home page, not to the pages that earn money.
Two pages trading one keywordDuplicate pages for one intent: due dates, calculators, addresses.No page has one clear job, so Google splits the signal and neither wins.
High impressions, low click-throughTitles and descriptions that do not carry the offer.The proof that sells (price, speed, credentials) lives on the page, not in the result.

Every problem gets two answers. Fixing only the first is why the same problem is back six months later.

How we run it

Fix the foundations. Then grow on top.

New content on a site Google half understands compounds slowly. The same content on a clean site compounds fast.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Diagnose

    Page by page: indexing, duplicates, internal links, schema, backlinks and an AI-answer baseline. Working, not working and why, agreed with your team.

  2. Weeks 3 to 4

    Fix

    Duplicates merged behind 301s, one keyword owner per page, titles rewritten to carry the proof, links pointed at the pages that earn.

  3. Month 2

    Structure

    A schema framework: organization, product, pricing, FAQ, how-to and article, with the same facts stated the same way everywhere.

  4. Month 3 on

    Grow

    Page-two keywords first, because they are closest to paying. Content in clusters that feed one service page. Links earned, never bought in spikes.

Where this pattern bites hardest

Three businesses we read differently.

  • Deadline and seasonal demand

    When the year's demand lands in a few weeks, month on month is noise. We compare year on year, and build before the peak so you enter it already ranking.

  • Commodity products near the price floor

    When the product is the same everywhere, search is where share is won: one price stated once, trust in the result itself, and an answer for every question the leader answers and you do not.

  • A network of sites and an in-house team

    We own the sites we are given, and write every tactic that works as a playbook with the return it produced, so your team can run it across the rest of the portfolio.
