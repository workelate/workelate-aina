The Brain / Changelog

What shipped, and when.

Every entry carries a date, and a date means the work runs on a real stack rather than existing in a plan. Newest first.

Book a demo What is being built next

Entries

Newest first.

If a capability is not dated here, it has not shipped: it is on the roadmap under building now or next, and we will say so plainly if you ask on a call. The capability inventory describes each entry in full.

11 September 2026Mail

Mail replies drafted with the context attached

  • WorkElate Chief, the Brain, reads a mail thread in full context and drafts the reply with the surrounding work attached.
  • A reply already knows the open items and what changed since the last exchange, so you edit a draft instead of reconstructing history.
11 September 2026Files

The folder is the container

  • One folder holds a client’s documents, sheets, decks, forms and boards together. It covers the applications that have told the Brain how they file, not yet all of them.
  • A question about that client reads the whole folder. Nothing was moved or migrated.
10 September 2026Decks

Deck registers and folder sources

  • A deck can be built from the files in a folder, so a review starts from the client's actual material.
  • The numbers on a slide come from the source that owns them, not from a summary, which is where figures drift.
6 September 2026Notice

Your systems read continuously

  • Connected applications report what they record, a ticket opened, a date moved, an invoice sent, without pause, so the morning list reflects this morning rather than an older picture.
  • Invisible when it works, which is why it is dated here.
UndatedStanding

Standing capability

  • One map of how your deals, threads, tickets, documents and people connect, and the ranked morning list, with one line saying why an item is on your list and one saying what it is waiting on.
  • Three questions before anyone is interrupted, capped so the same item is never raised twice.
  • Fifteen things it can be asked to do, with a stop in front of anything that sends, deletes or shares.
  • It checks a change landed before it says so, checks what it tells you against what happened, and says no when it cannot do something. All of it predates this page.
