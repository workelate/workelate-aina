FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Is Guru or WorkElate Chief better for a 500-person company?

If support keeps answering from a stale page, Guru, because verification and citations are its whole product. If the gap is a task nobody picked up or a client nobody chased, the Brain, because it briefs about deals, deliveries and invoices, not documents. The two rarely compete; decide which gap costs you more.

Can I use Guru and the Brain together?

Yes, where Guru records something the Brain can read. The Brain reads what a connected system emits, and a verified card in Guru is one such record; connecting it is engagement work in the Sprint, not a switch in a settings page.

What does Guru cost?

On request. Guru's pricing page says the price is scoped on the first call, so plan on a conversation before a number.

What does the Brain cost?

A two-week Diagnostic Sprint first, then a fixed build of $30K to $100K on your stack. The Sprint fee is not published; it is scoped on the first call, and the build price is fixed before it starts.
