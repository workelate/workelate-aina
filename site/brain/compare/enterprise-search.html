Compare

Search answers when asked. Then it stops at the answer.

Glean, Coveo, Guru, Microsoft Search, Google Cloud Search, Elastic. The most mature of the four categories, and the one we respect most, because permission aware retrieval across a real SaaS estate is hard engineering.

The short answer

If people cannot find what the company already knows, buy enterprise search. It deploys faster than we do, it is better than us at retrieval and synthesis, and it gives cited answers. The boundary is that search is summoned. It does not decide that something is worth interrupting you for, and it does not carry the work to done.
