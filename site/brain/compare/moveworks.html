The Brain / Compare / vs Moveworks

Moveworks answers you. The Brain watches work.

Moveworks is the assistant for your whole workforce. WorkElate Chief, the Brain, starts when something changes in your systems, not when an employee asks.

Book a demo See it running first

The short answer

For employee support at scale, Moveworks is the more mature product: an assistant in chat, web, intranet and mobile, in over 100 languages, with hundreds of plugins and connectors and an 8-week typical time to value the vendor publishes. Where we differ: Moveworks starts when an employee asks. The Brain starts when something changes in your systems, holds one memory of the work, and stops for a person before it changes anything.

Moveworks is the AI Assistant platform for your entire workforce to search and act across business applications, with a Reasoning Engine that autonomously understands, plans, executes and adapts, and Agent Studio for building agents across IT, HR, Finance and more. The Brain is WorkElate Chief, installed by WE_AINA on what you already run.
