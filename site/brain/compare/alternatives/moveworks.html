Questions

Moveworks alternatives, asked plainly

What is the closest Moveworks alternative for a mid-sized company?

Glean is the closest: an index over 275+ connectors with agents on top, answering the employee who asks. Guru is the closest for the answer itself, verified and cited into Slack and Teams. The Brain is the alternative when the work you care about never asks: it starts from what changed.

Is there a cheaper Moveworks alternative?

Moveworks prices by custom quote, so cheaper cannot be shown. Among the ten, Gemini is included from Workspace Business Starter, Microsoft 365 Copilot is $18 per user per month, Notion AI is $20 per member per month, and Dust starts at 24 euro per month. The Brain is a two-week Sprint, then $30K to $100K fixed.

Can the Brain replace Moveworks?

No. The Brain is not an employee help desk and does not answer a thousand IT questions a day in a hundred languages. It sits on the systems you already run, reads what changed, and asks a person before it writes. If tickets are the cost, Moveworks answers that better.
