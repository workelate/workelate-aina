Questions

Dust alternatives, asked plainly

What is the closest Dust alternative for a mid-sized company?

Notion AI is the closest: agents on triggers inside a workspace, at a published per-seat price. UnifyApps is the closest platform for a large enterprise. The Brain is the alternative when you want the judgment installed at a fixed fee, not a workbench your team staffs.

Is there a cheaper Dust alternative?

Dust publishes 24 euro per month for Pro, billed yearly, which is already among the lowest published prices of the ten. Microsoft 365 Copilot is $18 per user per month, Notion AI is $20 per member per month, and Gemini is included from Workspace Business Starter. The Brain is a two-week Sprint, then $30K to $100K fixed, not a per-seat price.

Can the Brain replace Dust?

No. The Brain is not a workbench and does not let your team build agents over twenty models. It sits on the systems you already run, reads what changed, and asks a person before it writes. If building is the point, Dust is the better buy.
