The Brain / Compare / vs Glean

Glean finds it. The Brain finishes it.

Glean is the strongest answer to “where is it” we know. WorkElate Chief, the Brain, starts from what changed and carries the work to done.

Book a demo See it running first

The short answer

If the complaint in your company is finding, buy Glean. Its index, its connector library and its source-system permission model are years ahead of anything we would build, and its agents can be triggered from key events and connected to external systems. Where we differ: the Brain does not start from the query but from what changed, a date moved or a thread gone quiet, holds one memory of the work across your systems, and waits for a person's yes before anything that matters.

Glean calls itself an enterprise AI platform connected to your enterprise's data, with 275+ connectors and permission-aware search where users only see what they are allowed to see. The Brain is WorkElate Chief, installed by WE_AINA on what you already run.
