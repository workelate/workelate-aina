FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Is an iPaaS or RPA platform or WorkElate Chief better for a 500-person company?

If the path is known and never varies, write the flow; where regulation requires the same action every time, a judgment layer is a liability. If cases keep arriving that no flow was written for, the Brain, because it judges what needs a person and calls your flows as the hands. Most 500-person companies keep the flows and add the judging.

Can I use an iPaaS or RPA platform and the Brain together?

Yes, where the platform records something the Brain can read. The Brain reads what a connected system emits, and a recipe run or an automation is one such event; connecting it is engagement work in the Sprint, not a switch in a settings page.

What does an iPaaS or RPA platform cost?

On request. Workato publishes a pricing page without a price, and we found no public pricing page for UnifyApps.

What does the Brain cost?

A two-week Diagnostic Sprint first, then a fixed build of $30K to $100K on your stack. The Sprint fee is not published; it is scoped on the first call, and the build price is fixed before it starts.
