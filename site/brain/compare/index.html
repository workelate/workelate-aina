By name

Ten products you may already be looking at

Each page names what the product is better at before it names where we differ. Grounded on the vendor's own public pages, with the sources listed.

Sometimes one of them is the right buy and we are not. WorkElate Chief, the Brain, is one memory across the systems a 200 to 2,000 person company already runs, made by WorkElate and installed by WE_AINA, WorkElate's own studio: it reads what your CRM, mail, tickets and documents already record, judges what needs a person, and asks before it changes anything.

See a workflow end to end →

01

Glean

Better at finding anything across 275+ connectors with permissions honored. We differ: we start from what changed, keep one memory, and wait for a person before anything sends.

 02

Microsoft 365 Copilot

Better inside Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams, grounded in Microsoft Graph, at a published price. We differ: one memory across the suite and everything next to it.

 03

Gemini for Google Workspace

Better in every Workspace app you open, included in the Business plans. We differ: the record that lives outside Workspace is still ours to watch.

 04

UnifyApps

Better as an AI operating system for a large enterprise, on-prem if you need it. We differ: a fixed-fee install for 200 to 2,000 people, not a platform to staff.

 05

Moveworks

Better at answering every employee in every channel and language. We differ: we watch the work that goes quiet, not the queue that asks.

 06

Notion AI

Better as a teammate inside the Notion page, with agents on triggers at a published price. We differ: one memory across systems, not one workspace.

 07

Guru

Better at keeping knowledge verified, with a citation on every answer. We differ: we keep the work moving, not the wiki true.

 08

Dust

Better as a workbench where your team builds its own agents over 20+ models. We differ: the judgment is installed, not assembled.

 09

Coveo

Better at relevance for commerce, service and workplace search. We differ: we judge the next move, not the ranking.

 10

Workato

Better at running a known process exactly once across 12,000+ connectors, with approvals on consequential actions. We differ: we judge the case with no recipe.

Comparison based on publicly available product information as of September 2026, not a controlled test. If we have something wrong, tell us and we will fix it.
