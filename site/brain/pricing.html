The Brain / Pricing

An engagement, not a seat

Two priced things,
and you own what we build.

On Monday this means you are not modelling a per seat licence and you are not guessing what the third year looks like. There are two things to buy. A two week Diagnostic Sprint at a fixed fee, whose output is a build plan with a price on it. Then a build, fixed price, $30K–$100K, scoped before work starts. If we misjudged the shape of the work, we absorb it. At the end you own the code, the data and the system, and you can fire us and keep all of it.

01 · The shape

Why this is not sold per seat

A seat price assumes the value is a person opening an application. The value here is a handoff that stops being a person, so the unit is the workflow.

01

You buy a workflow finished, not a licence to try

One workflow, crossing at least two systems, running in one team. Small enough that being wrong costs eight weeks, large enough to move a number somebody tracks.

02

Fixed price, quoted before work starts

No day rate, no time and materials tail. The Sprint is the discovery, and it is priced.

03

Scope creep is our problem

If the work is bigger than we judged, we absorb it. A fixed price with a change order process is a day rate in disguise.
