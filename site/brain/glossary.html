The Brain / Glossary

The words we use on purpose.

These pages use a small set of words precisely, and this page says what each one means. Where a word means something narrower here than elsewhere, the narrower meaning governs.

Book a demo The FAQ

01 · The system

The system itself

TERM

The Brain

The layer that holds one memory of work crossing several systems, judges what deserves attention, and acts with a human yes in front of anything it changes.

TERM

Chief

The product name of the Brain, WorkElate Chief, our own software. The Brain and Chief mean the same thing here.

TERM

Work graph

One map of how your deals, threads, tickets, documents and people connect.

TERM

Business object

One kind of thing it can act on: a document, a deck, a sheet tab, a mail thread, a commitment.

TERM

Organization memory

The facts it keeps about one company, each stamped with which company it belongs to before it can be stored.

TERM

Decision history

The kept record of what was approved, declined and left alone, read back the next time something similar happens.

02 · Sensing

How it comes to know anything

TERM

Signal

What your tools already record as work happens: a ticket opened, a date moved, an invoice sent. A signal reports; it never decides.

TERM

Declared event

An event an application tells the Brain it records. What a system reports is what the Brain can see; nothing is scraped.

TERM

Folder scope

Reading everything filed against one client or engagement as one subtree, rather than one application’s slice of it.

TERM

Document grounding

Reading the contract or the written policy at the moment of the decision, so the same change can be judged differently for two clients whose terms differ.

TERM

Judgment

A decision made by reasoning over context against a written policy, as opposed to a rule firing.

03 · Deciding

How it decides whether to speak

TERM

Interruption budget

A cap on how often it may interrupt: 40 an hour company-wide, 5 per person, changed by saying so.

TERM

Cooldown

The minimum interval before the same item may be raised again. A hundred submissions to one form produce one item that updates.

TERM

Ranked briefing

The short ordered list a person meets in the morning, composed by judgment rather than by sorting a feed.

TERM

Reason line

The sentence saying why an item is on your list. An alert says a rule fired; a reason line says what was concluded and from what.

TERM

Blocker line

The sentence saying what is standing in the way of the item moving, and who or what it is waiting on.

TERM

Honest refusal

It says no, and says what is missing, instead of quietly doing the nearest thing.

04 · Acting

What it is allowed to do

TERM

Verb

One of the fifteen things it can be asked to do: find, read, research, recall, remember, ask, create, edit, improve, delete, send, share, schedule, assign, judge.

TERM

Action

One concrete thing a verb turns into, in one application against one kind of thing.

TERM

Confirm gate

The stop in front of a consequential action, where it waits for a person to click yes. The AI cannot open it.

TERM

Destructive action

An action that requires that yes: anything that sends, deletes or shares, by category.

TERM

Read after write

The first check between doing and saying: it checks the change actually landed, inside your company’s own data, before telling you it did.

TERM

Claim check

The second check: what it tells you is checked against what actually happened, not against what you asked for.
