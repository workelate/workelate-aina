The Brain / Glossary

The vocabulary, defined once

The words
we use on purpose.

Most vendor glossaries exist to make ordinary things sound technical. This one exists for the opposite reason: these pages use a small set of words precisely, and a reader who takes a verb to mean a feature, or judgment to mean a rule, will misread everything downstream. Where a word means something narrower here than elsewhere, the narrower meaning governs.

01 · The system

The system itself

TERM

The brain

The layer that holds one memory of work crossing several systems, judges what deserves attention, and acts with a human yes in front of anything it changes.

TERM

Chief

The product name of the brain, WorkElate Chief, our own software. The Brain and Chief mean the same thing here.

TERM

Work graph

Typed links between the things work is made of: a deal, a thread, a ticket, a document, a task, a person.

TERM

Business object

One addressable noun the system can act on: a document, a deck, a sheet tab, a mail thread, a commitment. There are 190.

TERM

Organization memory

The durable facts held about one organization, each naming the organization it belongs to before it can be stored.

TERM

Decision history

The kept record of what was approved, declined and left alone, read back the next time something similar happens.

02 · Sensing

How it comes to know anything

TERM

Signal

A statement that something happened in a connected system, emitted as work occurs. A signal reports; it never decides.

TERM

Declared event

An event type an application announces about itself. What a system emits is what the brain can see.

TERM

Folder scope

Reading everything filed against one client or engagement as one subtree, rather than one application’s slice of it.

TERM

Document grounding

Reading the contract or the written policy at the moment of the decision, so the same change can be judged differently for two clients whose terms differ.

TERM

Judgment

A decision made by reasoning over context against a written policy, as opposed to a rule firing.

03 · Deciding

How it decides whether to speak

TERM

Interruption budget

At most 40 interruptions per organization per hour and 5 per person per hour by default, changed by saying so.

TERM

Cooldown

The minimum interval before the same item may be raised again. A hundred submissions to one form produce one item that updates.

TERM

Ranked briefing

The short ordered list a person meets in the morning, composed by judgment rather than by sorting a feed.

TERM

Reason line

The sentence saying why an item is on your list. An alert says a rule fired; a reason line says what was concluded and from what.

TERM

Blocker line

The sentence saying what is standing in the way of the item moving, and who or what it is waiting on.

TERM

Honest refusal

Saying plainly that a request cannot be served, instead of narrating a success on the nearest half fitting action.

04 · Acting

What it is allowed to do

TERM

Verb

One of the 15 things the model may want: find, read, research, recall, remember, ask, create, edit, improve, delete, send, share, schedule, assign, judge.

TERM

Action

One concrete thing a verb resolves to, on a specific surface against a specific object. There are 287.

TERM

Confirm gate

The stop in front of a consequential action, waiting for a person to say yes. A model cannot open it.

TERM

Destructive action

An action that requires that confirmation, 71 of the 287. Delete and send are destructive by category.

TERM

Read after write

The first check between doing and saying: a write is re-read inside your organization’s fence before it is believed.

TERM

Claim check

The second check: the drafted sentence is compared against the raw data the tools returned, not against what you asked for.
