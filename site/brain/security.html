The Brain / Security

For the person who has to say no

Identity comes from the session,
never from the model.

This is the page to forward to whoever runs your security review. On Monday it changes one thing: instead of a vendor questionnaire full of adjectives, your security lead gets a short list of mechanisms, a short list of limits, and a straight no where the answer is no. We hold no certification and we do not display one.

Book a Diagnostic Sprint How it is built What is not proven

01 · The threat we designed against

Anything a model can write, a document can influence

The interesting attack is an instruction hidden in a document or an email that persuades the model to name a different organization or user.

01

Identity is resolved from the verified session

Identity and token fields are stripped from anything the model produces before an action runs. The real organization and user come from the session.

02

A tool cannot read its own approval off the model’s arguments

Consent is recognised only from a door where a person clicked. An approval field in model output is not consent.

03

Credentials are read at request time

Never cached at start, so a revoked credential takes effect at the next call, not the next restart.

02 · The fence and the gate

What it may read, and what it may change on its own

The fence

Scope is a property of the record, not of the query

The organization is on the record and in the index, every memory fact must name its organization, and retrieval is fenced on the same field.

The unknown

An unfenced check returns unknown, never a pass

If the fence cannot be applied, no query runs. It is never re run without the fence to get an answer.

The gate

71 of 287 actions stop for a person

Delete and send are gated by category. The system cannot approve itself, and the reason is structural.

The claim

What it says it did is checked against what happened

A write is re-read inside your fence before it is believed, and the drafted sentence is compared against what the tools returned. Both abstain when uncertain.

04 · What we do not claim

The honest section

01

No certification, of any kind

No SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no HIPAA posture, no badge. If procurement requires one before a pilot, we cannot pass that gate today.

02

Data handling terms are agreed per engagement, in writing

Where data sits, who may reach it, how long it is kept and what happens on exit. A page is not what you will sign.

03

Anything not measured is named as not measured

Uptime, latency and accuracy at your scale are absent because nobody has measured them for your deployment.
