Use case · the board pack

Four days to build a pack
that is out of date when it is read.

The monthly or quarterly pack, crossing the finance system, the project tool, the CRM, spreadsheets and the deck. Every number in it already exists somewhere. The four days are transcription.

What changes at close: the figures come from the systems that hold them and stay pointed at them.

Book a Diagnostic Sprint The other three workflows See it running first

The same month, with one memory

Pulled, compared, explained, and approved by a person

Day 1, 09:00

The pull starts from the sources

Each figure is read from the system that owns it. Nothing is exported to a staging sheet, nothing is retyped.

Day 1, 09:05

Every figure arrives with its previous period beside it

Prior-period figures come from the same source, not last month’s deck, so a restatement shows up instead of freezing.

Day 1, 11:30

The movements come with the work behind them

Margin down two points arrives next to the scope change, the slipped delivery and the changed document.

Day 1, 14:00 · a person confirms

The CFO accepts nine figures and rejects one

Each figure is shown with its source before it enters the pack. The rejection is remembered for next period.

Day 1, 14:20

The deck is built from the folder the work lives in

The folder holding the quarter holds the sheets and the approved figures. The chart on slide four is the sheet, not a picture.

Day 1, 15:40 · a person confirms

Sharing the pack waits for a click

Sharing and sending are among the 71 actions that wait for a human confirmation.

What had to be true for this to work

THE CONNECTED SYSTEMS

THE CONFIRM GATE

No pack ships without a click

71 of the 287 actions wait for a person.

THE INTERRUPTION BUDGET

Close week is not a storm

At most 40 interruptions per organization per hour and 5 per person.

What this does not do

It does not replace your finance system, your CRM or your close process.

It does not produce a figure it cannot source.
