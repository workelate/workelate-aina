Use cases

Depth is a workflow finished,
not a list of apps connected.

Anyone can count integrations. The question is how far into one real piece of work an assistant can go before it hands the work back to you. Here are four, walked from the first signal to the last write, with the moment a person had to say yes.

The four

Pick the one that looks like your Tuesday

All four share one shape: the work crosses systems, the Brain folds what those systems already emit into one work graph, and every write waits for a person. 71 of its 287 actions stop for a click, so the worst case is a suggestion someone declines.

DELIVERY

Client delivery, end to end

Crosses a project tool, chat, mail, documents and a calendar. The tell is a calendar full of reviews that each cost an assembly job before anyone speaks.

FINANCE

Order to cash

Crosses the order system, dispatch, the invoice and the bank feed. The tell is a person opening three screens to agree that one invoice matches one delivery.

OPERATIONS

The shared inbox as a queue

Crosses mail, the CRM, the ticket tool and the task board. The tell is an inbox two people watch and neither owns.

THE BOARD PACK

Monthly and quarterly reporting

Crosses the finance system, the project tool, the CRM, spreadsheets and the deck. The tell is a pack that takes four days and is out of date when read.
