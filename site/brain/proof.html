01 · The product

The vocabulary, and what it reaches

Fifteen things it can be asked to do, not hundreds of buttons: find, read, research, recall, remember, ask, create, edit, improve, delete, send, share, schedule, assign, judge. The security page covers the mechanisms.

15

Things it can be asked to do.

287

Actions those fifteen reach across our own 12 applications.

190

Kinds of thing it can act on, from document to commitment.

71

Actions that send, delete or share, and stop for a person.

The product’s own counts, on our own applications; the Sprint establishes which of your systems it reaches. Interruptions are capped at 40 an hour company-wide, 5 per person.

03 · The gaps

What is not proven

This section is the reason to believe the rest of the page.

01

Third-party connectors are not built

They sit on the roadmap, intended and not started.

02

No certification is claimed

No SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no badge; data handling terms are agreed per engagement, in writing.

03

The counts describe our own apps, not your systems

They prove what the product can reach, not what has been exercised against your systems.

04

Nothing here measures performance at your scale

Uptime, latency and accuracy are absent because nobody has measured them for your deployment.

05

We do not claim to mirror your permission model

Reads stay inside your company’s own data; matching each user’s source-system permissions is scoped per engagement.

06

Where a thing only exists in code, we say so

What has shipped is dated on the changelog; anything undated has not shipped.
