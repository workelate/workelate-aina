01 · The product

The vocabulary, and what it reaches

The model is handed 15 verbs rather than hundreds of buttons: find, read, research, recall, remember, ask, create, edit, improve, delete, send, share, schedule, assign, judge. That number only ever falls.

15

Verbs exposed to the model.

287

Concrete actions those verbs resolve to, across the Brain’s own 12 application surfaces.

190

Distinct business objects addressed: a document, a deck, a sheet tab, a calendar slot, a mail thread, a commitment.

71

Actions classified destructive, each waiting for a human yes.

These are the product’s own counts and they change as it does. The 12 surfaces are the Brain’s own; which of your systems they reach is what the Sprint establishes. Interruptions are budgeted at 40 per organization and 5 per person per hour by default.

03 · The gaps

What is not proven

This section is the reason to believe the rest of the page. Diligence would find each item anyway.

01

Third party connectors are not built

None has been built. They sit on the roadmap as intended and not started.

02

No certification is claimed

No SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no badge. Data handling terms are agreed per engagement, in writing.

03

The counts describe our surfaces, not your systems

They prove what the product can reach, not that any of it has been exercised against your estate.

04

Nothing here measures performance at your scale

Uptime, latency and accuracy percentages are absent because nobody has measured them for your deployment.

05

We do not claim to mirror your permission model

We stand behind organization level scoping. Per user permission fidelity is a harder claim, scoped per engagement.

06

Built, wired and proven are different words here

Where a thing is only built we say built. What shipped is dated on the changelog.
