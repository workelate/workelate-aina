The Brain / Capabilities / Interruption budget

SENSE

A brain that interrupts freely
is worse than no brain at all.

At most 40 interruptions per organization per hour and 5 per person per hour by default, with cooldowns so the same item never arrives twice. Once a system can see every change and every silence across a company, correctness is not the constraint. Attention is.

What changes on Monday: the assistant you switched off for talking too much is replaced by one that is rationed, and the numbers are yours to change by saying so.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

A busy hour, and the things that were not said

14:00

Thirty-one candidates across five systems

Crosses the project tool, mail, documents, the calendar and finance. Every one is true and defensible. This is the hour that ruins an unbudgeted assistant.

14:01

Nineteen are the same item wearing different hats

Crosses the project tool and mail. One moved date produced eleven candidates; one quiet thread produced eight. They collapse to two items, each raised once and updated in place.

14:03

Three people get one item each, and two wait

Crosses mail and the project tool. The account manager has already had five pushes this hour, so their item queues. Nobody is told that thirty-one things happened.

How it works

Three mechanisms

01

Two ceilings, counted centrally

40 per organization and 5 per person per hour, counted in one place so it is one real cap. A ceiling is not a target; judgment raises far fewer in practice.

02

Cooldowns, so an item is raised once

Within its window the same item is updated in place rather than raised again. This is what makes silence detection survivable, because a gap that grows every hour would otherwise ring every hour.

03

The numbers are sentences, not constants

Each limit is a preference stored with the sentence that states it. Fewer pushes at month end, nothing to finance before ten: said once, changed by saying otherwise.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a guarantee the right thing is inside the budget. A ration makes noise survivable; it does not make judgment perfect.

It is not a do-not-disturb switch on your own tools. It governs what the Brain says, not your colleagues.

It does not replace the confirm gate. The 71 confirm-gated actions stay gated however much budget is left.
