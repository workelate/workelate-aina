The Brain / Capabilities / Decision history

RECALL

You should never have to explain
the same context twice.

Most assistants start every session from an empty prompt, so the person pays the context tax again and eventually stops bothering. Decision history writes back what was decided, when and on what grounds, and the next decision opens with it in hand.

What changes on Monday: the hundredth answer is better than the first, because the reason stayed next to the decision instead of walking out of the room.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

The second discount request of the quarter

14:20

The prior decision comes back with its grounds

Crosses mail, documents and memory. Nine weeks ago a twelve percent discount was declined here because the contract ties discount to a committed volume. That sentence is the useful part; declined alone is noise.

14:21

The draft starts from the argument, not the question

Crosses documents and the finance system. The reply names the earlier decision and points out the one thing that changed: the volume clause was met last quarter.

14:24

The approval is given, and the reason is written back

Crosses mail and memory. The lead approves and adds one sentence about a competitive situation. The third request opens with two decisions and two reasons. That is the compounding.

How it works

Writing back is a step in the loop, not a feature bolted on

01

Remember is one of the 15 verbs

Writing a decision back is an ordinary action with the same fences and addressing as any other, not a side effect somebody hoped would happen.

02

A decision is stored against a subject

Every fact names what it is about and hangs off the client, project or document. Recall reaches it by walking from the subject of today’s question, so nobody has to remember to search.

03

A decline is kept as carefully as an approval

71 of the 287 actions stop for a human confirmation, which produces a steady stream of declines. A decline is a person telling you where the judgment was wrong, in context, for free.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a policy engine. A prior decision is evidence, never a rule that forces the same answer twice.

It cannot record a decision nobody made in a system.

It does not improve in a quiet quarter. Compounding needs decisions flowing through it.
