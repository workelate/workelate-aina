The Brain / Capabilities / Multi-step sequences

ACT

Four steps is still one piece of work.
It should not be four prompts.

An assistant that does one thing per instruction leaves the hardest part with you: holding the sequence. The Brain runs a compound request as one turn across its own 12 application surfaces, and what each step returns decides the next.

What changes on Monday: you say the outcome once, and the Brain carries it across surfaces, stopping in front of the step that has consequences rather than every step.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

One sentence at 08:58, done by 09:05

08:58

It finds the three quiet renewals, and reads what quiet means

One has had no reply for nineteen days, one is waiting on a legal redline, one is quiet because its review is already booked. Same signal, three situations.

09:02

Two drafts, each written from a different reason

The nineteen-day account gets a chase naming the last thing agreed. The redline account gets a note to the internal owner. The third is reported as handled, not silently dropped.

09:05 · a person confirms

She approves the sending step, and only that step

Reading, assembling and drafting ran without asking. One confirmation covers the outbound half and holds the two follow-up slots. Seven minutes, one click.

How it works

Three mechanisms

01

One loop, not a workflow builder

Sense, recall, reason, decide, act, remember. Nothing about the sequence above was configured in advance, so a request nobody anticipated behaves like one seen a hundred times.

02

A small vocabulary is what makes composition possible

15 verbs resolving to 287 actions means every step is drawn from one set that reaches everywhere, so any step can hand its result to any other.

03

Length does not buy autonomy

Each write is re-read before the next step leans on it, and 71 of the 287 actions still stop for a person. Being step four of four is not an exemption.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a scheduler for long-running processes. A sequence is one piece of work in one turn, not a job that checks in for three days.

It does not guess past an ambiguous instruction. Where the request could mean two things, it asks.

It reaches only the surfaces the engagement connected, and it works best on an outcome stated plainly rather than an instruction written like a ticket.
