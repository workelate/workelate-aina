The Brain / Capabilities / Calendar awareness

SENSE

Work becomes urgent
before the meeting asks.

WorkElate Chief, the Brain, ties work to the meeting that will ask about it: urgency rises as it nears, and drops after.

Book a demo See it running first

What it looks like on a Tuesday

A Thursday board review, seen from Tuesday

08:05

Thursday’s review pulls six items forward

The deck references three numbers, two projects and one open decision. All six move inside a forty-eight hour horizon.

08:06

One of the six is not ready

Not prepare for the review, but: the number on slide four is eleven days old, and its source changed.

Thursday 11:05

After the meeting, urgency evaporates

The six items fall back to their own weight. One gained a new date in the room. Nobody turned alarms off.

How it works

Three mechanisms

01

A meeting is a record, not a notification

Calendar events sit in the work graph, the map linking tasks, documents, accounts and meetings, so work can inherit their dates.

02

Urgency is derived, and it decays

Nothing is stamped urgent. A linked meeting’s nearness is weighed on every read, and after the meeting that input is gone.

03

Scheduling is still a confirmed action

Booking, moving or cancelling a meeting stops for a person to click yes, and the AI cannot approve its own action.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It does not hunt for slots or move meetings.

An invitation with no agenda is just a rectangle.

It knows nothing said in the room unless written down.
