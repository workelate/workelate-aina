The Brain / Capabilities / Calendar awareness

SENSE

The meeting is the real deadline.
Your task list has never known that.

Almost nothing is due on the date in the due-date field. It is due before the call where somebody will ask about it. Calendar awareness attaches work to the meetings that will interrogate it, so urgency rises as the meeting approaches and drops the moment it is over.

What changes on Monday: the list re-sorts itself as the week moves, and nobody spends an hour re-prioritising it by hand.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

A Thursday board review, seen from Tuesday morning

08:05

Thursday’s review pulls six items forward

Crosses the calendar, the project tool and documents. The deck references three numbers, two projects and one open decision. All six are now inside a forty-eight hour horizon because Thursday will ask about them.

08:06

One of the six is not ready, and says why

Crosses documents and the finance system. The item raised is not prepare for the review. It is the number on slide four is eleven days old and its source changed on the ninth.

Thursday 11:05

After the meeting, the urgency evaporates

Crosses the calendar and the project tool. The six items fall back to their own weight. One gained a new date in the room. Nobody turned the alarms off.

How it works

Three mechanisms

01

A meeting is a first-class record, not a notification

Calendar events sit in the same work graph as tasks, documents and accounts, connected by typed relations. Without those relations a calendar is a list of rectangles and nothing can inherit anything.

02

Urgency is derived at read time, and it decays

Nothing is stamped urgent. Proximity of a linked meeting is one input weighed on every read, and once the meeting has happened the input is gone, so the item falls on its own.

03

Scheduling anything is still a confirmed action

Reading the diary and writing to it are different privileges. Booking, moving or cancelling is among the 71 confirm-gated actions, and a model cannot approve its own action.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a scheduling assistant. It does not hunt for slots or move meetings, and nothing writes to a diary without a person confirming.

It cannot connect work to a meeting whose invitation says nothing. Two initials and no agenda is a rectangle.

It does not know what was said in the room unless somebody writes the outcome down.
