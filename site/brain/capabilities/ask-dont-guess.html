The Brain / Capabilities / Ask, do not guess

REASON

Two possible meanings, one instruction.
It asks which.

A confident wrong answer costs more than a question. So when a request could mean two things, WorkElate Chief, the Brain, asks, instead of taking the likelier reading and acting on it.

What changes on Monday: the failure mode you meet is a clarifying question, not a message sent to the wrong client.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

Three ambiguities and what each one gets.

10:20

Two clients share a name

Crosses mail and a finance sheet. The instruction was chase Henderson. Two accounts match, one thirty days late and one current. It asks which, and shows both so the answer is a click.

14:05

It is asked for something it does not have

Crosses nothing. Last quarter’s churn by segment, and no connected system holds segment. The answer names what is missing and what it could give instead. It does not assemble a plausible table.

14:06

Clear instructions are not interrogated

Crosses documents and the project tool. The same hour, eleven unambiguous requests run without a question. A system that hedges constantly cannot tell the hard cases from the easy ones.

How it works

Three mechanisms that make abstaining safe to build.

01

Ambiguity is judged, not pattern matched

Whether a word is ambiguous depends on what is in your data this morning, not on a list of risky phrases. Two Hendersons make one word ambiguous; one Henderson makes it precise.

02

Refusing is never the same as being unable

The Brain may not deny a capability it has. When it says no, it names the missing input or connection, so a no never becomes an excuse for a system that is quietly broken.

03

The stop before a send is the second net, not the first

Anything that sends, deletes or shares stops for a person, and the AI can never approve its own action. Asking first means fewer of those stops are a person catching a mistake.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a guarantee of never being wrong. It reduces confident wrongness; it does not abolish it.

It cannot ask about an ambiguity it cannot see.

It does not ask outside the company. A question always comes back to a person here, never to your client.
