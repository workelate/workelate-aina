What it looks like when it fires

The second alert that never arrives

01

A milestone date moves

The plan app reports it, it is judged worth one interruption inside the cap of 40 an hour and 5 per person, and raised in the account’s thread.

02

The manager answers in one line

“The client asked for this, the new date is agreed, do not chase it,” and the answer is remembered against the account.

03

The next signal reads that answer

Two days later a downstream task slips for the same reason; the judgment finds the agreed date and stays quiet.

How it works

One loop, entered from two doors

01

An event and a question share one path

There is no second pipeline with its own rules, so a change to how things are judged applies to both doors.

02

The thread is keyed to the work

Come back on Thursday from another device and the account’s thread is where it was, nudges and answers included.

03

One budget governs both doors

The same hourly cap applies to both, and changes when you say so in plain words.

What it is not

Three honest limits

Not a replacement for your chat tool.

It cannot remember what was never said to it.
