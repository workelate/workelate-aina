The Brain / Capabilities / One continuous thread

CONNECT

The nudge it raised and the question you asked
are the same conversation.

Most assistants have two personalities: a chat window that forgets last session, and a notification with no idea what you already said. Here a signal and a typed question enter the same loop, read the same memory, and land in the same thread.

What changes on Monday: you reply to an alert with “already handled, the client moved the date”, and the second alert about the same date does not arrive.

What it looks like when it fires

The second alert that never arrives

01

A milestone date moves, and it is raised in the thread

The application that owns the plan declares the change. It is judged worth one interruption, inside the 40 per organization and 5 per person hourly budget, and raised in the account’s thread.

02

The manager answers in one line

“The client asked for this, the new date is agreed, do not chase it.” In most systems that is a dismissal that teaches nothing. Here it is remembered against the account.

03

The next signal on the same date is judged against that answer

Two days later a downstream task slips for the same reason. The judgment reads the memory, finds the agreed date, and stays quiet. Silence was earned by a sentence, not a suppression rule.

How it works

One loop, entered from two doors

01

A signal and a question take the same path

Recall, reason, decide, act with a confirm, remember. There is no second pipeline with its own rules, so a change to how things are judged applies to both doors at once.

02

The thread is keyed to the work, not to a session

Conversation attaches to the thing being worked on. Come back on Thursday from a different device and the account’s thread is where it was, nudges and answers included.

03

One budget governs both, so the two cannot gang up on you

The 40 per organization and 5 per person hourly ceilings apply to the whole loop, and change when you say so in plain words. Two polite subsystems is how eleven interruptions happen before lunch.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a replacement for your chat tool. It is one thread about the work; people keep talking wherever they already do.

It cannot remember what was never said to it. A correction given verbally in a standup is invisible to it.

Continuity is scoped to what an engagement connects, and it runs on the nudges you answer in a sentence rather than close.
