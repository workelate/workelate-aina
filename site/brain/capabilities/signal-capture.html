The Brain / Capabilities / Signal capture

SENSE

Your systems already say what happened.
Almost nothing is listening.

Every tool your company runs announces its own events: a task created, a document shared, an invoice drafted. Signal capture reads those announcements as they are emitted, so the Brain is told what happened instead of polling a stale copy.

What changes on Monday: what moved on a client is answered from events that already arrived, not from a crawl that starts when you ask.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

Four minutes in the life of one client account

09:04

A delivery lead renames a milestone

Crosses the project tool. One event: the record, the old name, the new name, the person, the time. The Brain now holds a milestone one week later than the signed scope says.

09:06

Finance drafts an invoice on the same account

Crosses the finance system, through a connection built during the engagement. The Brain does not copy your ledger. It holds the fact that an invoice was drafted at 09:06, and the pointer back.

09:08

The account becomes answerable

Crosses all three. Ask at 14:00 what changed on this client today: no crawl, no waiting. The answer was assembled as the day happened.

How it works

Three mechanisms, none of them a crawler

01

Applications declare their own events

Each application announces what it emits and what each event carries. The Brain holds no list of applications, so adding a system is that system’s own declaration, not a change to the Brain.

02

Push, not poll, and never a screen

No screen reader, no browser agent, no scheduled scrape. A redesign of a tool you run breaks nothing here, and a signal arrives in the same second as the work.

03

Every signal is fenced to one organization

A signal carries the organization it belongs to. Identity and token fields are stripped from anything a model produces before an action runs, so identity always comes from the authenticated session.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not employee monitoring. It reads business records changing, not keystrokes or time at a desk.

It is not a universal connector. Each third-party system is connected deliberately during an engagement, and until then the Brain says it cannot see it.

It cannot see a change no system announces. Work agreed on a call and written nowhere is as invisible to the Brain as it is to you.
