The Brain / Capabilities

Every capability, in plain language.

What WorkElate Chief, the Brain, notices, what it remembers, what it judges, what it is allowed to touch, and what it is allowed to say it did. Nothing here requires you to move your data anywhere.

Six things it does

What the Brain, WorkElate Chief, does inside a 200 to 2,000 person company.

Each card names the work it removes. How it is built explains the one process behind all six.

SENSE

It notices what changed without being asked.

It reads what your systems already record, a ticket opened, a date moved, an invoice sent, and treats a thread that has gone quiet as an event too. There is a cap on how often it may interrupt: 40 an hour company-wide, 5 per person, changed in plain English.

RECALL

It gathers the whole picture before it forms an opinion.

The answer to almost every real question lives in four systems at once. It holds one map of how your deals, threads, tickets, documents and people connect, treats four spellings of a client as one client, remembers what was decided last time, and reads the contract at decision time.

REASON

It judges each case, instead of running a rule.

A rule per scenario rots, because nobody can list next quarter’s situations. The Brain weighs the context against what this role cares about, asks when an instruction is ambiguous, and says plainly what needs a person. Most of the time the right answer is silence.

ACT

It does the work, and a person decides.

It can be asked to do fifteen things, from find and draft to send and assign. Those reach 287 actions inside our own apps, and 71 of them, everything that sends, deletes or shares, stop for a person. Which of your systems it reaches is what the two-week Sprint establishes.

PROVE

It only claims what actually happened.

If an AI assistant disappointed you before, this is where it happened: it told you something was done. Here it checks the change actually landed before telling you it did, checks its reply against what the tools returned, and when it cannot do something it says no, and says what is missing.

CONNECT AND GOVERN

Nothing is replaced, and it can only act as the person who signed in.

Your systems stay where they are and stay the ones that own the data. Nothing a document or the AI writes can change who is asking, a twelfth system costs what the second did, and what it remembers is kept inside your company’s own data, never used as training material.

The full set

Every capability, on its own page.

One page each: what it looks like on a Tuesday, how it works, and what it is not. The list is generated from those pages, so a capability cannot quietly fall out of it.

SENSE

What it notices without being asked

01

It finds who really holds it, and tells them.

A message to a channel of twelve people is a message to nobody.

 02

It interrupts anyone only a few times an hour.

The interruption budget is a cap on how often the Brain may interrupt anyone: at most 40 times an hour across the company and 5 per person, and the same item is never raised twice.

 03

It listens to what your systems already record.

Every tool your company runs already records its own events: a task created, a document shared, an invoice drafted.

 04

It notices when nothing has happened for too long.

A thread quiet for nine days.

 05

It says what changed , not just that something did.

A notification says something moved, and you still open the tool to find out what.

 06

Work becomes urgent before the meeting asks.

Almost nothing is due on the date in the due-date field.
RECALL

One memory where there used to be five

01

Ask about a client, get the client, not documents.

Your systems each hold a true fragment of the same engagement, and none holds the link between the fragments.

 02

Four spellings of one client, treated as one client.

Northbeam Ltd in finance, Northbeam Limited on the contract, NORTHBEAM in the ticket queue, northbeam-2019 as a folder.

 03

It remembers why, so you never explain twice.

Most assistants start every conversation from nothing, so the person pays the context tax again and eventually stops bothering.

 04

One client’s question reads that client’s folder, nothing else.

An operating system folder does not ask which application you are in before showing what is inside it.

 05

Your company’s memory stays your company’s.

Every fact it keeps is stamped with whose it is, and it only ever reads yours.

 06

Your contract, read at decision time, not typed-in rules.

Every automation project reaches the same fork: turn the agreement into rules that rot the day it is amended, or read the agreement itself every time.
REASON

Judgment, not rules

01

A short morning list, each item with its reason.

The morning list is the screen most people meet first, so it decides whether anybody trusts the rest.

 02

Change its behaviour by saying so in plain words.

Tell it nothing before nine, and that is the setting.

 03

Related work and the contract, read before answering.

The answer to a serious question lives in four places: the work next to it, the thread about it, the decision taken in March, and the document that says what you agreed.

 04

Small things run fast. Consequential things stop for you.

How much thinking a decision gets depends on what happens if it is wrong.

 05

The same event ranks differently for each person.

A milestone slipping two days is the delivery lead’s first line of the morning.

 06

Three questions before it interrupts anyone.

Interrupting a person is the expensive part.

 07

Two possible meanings, one instruction. It asks which.

A confident wrong answer costs more than a question.
ACT

It does the work, and a person decides

01

Fifteen things it does. The list only gets shorter.

Find, read, draft, send, assign: fifteen verbs are the whole of what the Brain can be asked to do, and they reach every one of our own apps.

 02

Filed where the next question finds it.

Most assistants can make you a deck, which then lives wherever the tool put it, disconnected from the client.

 03

One date changes everywhere, and it checks it did.

A date moves in a meeting and reaches the plan, the tracker and the forecast through three people at three different times.

 04

One request, several steps, one confirmation.

An assistant that does one thing per instruction leaves the hardest part with you: holding the sequence.

 05

The move proposed, evidence beside it. A person confirms.

The work moved on Thursday.

 06

The reply is already drafted. You read and decide.

Most knowledge work is not writing.

 07

Whatever sends, deletes or shares stops for a person.

71 of the 287 actions the Brain can take reach outside: send, delete, share, anything that lands on a person rather than a row.
PROVE

What it is allowed to claim

01

It checks the change landed before it says so.

Every action that changes something returns a result saying whether it worked, written by the same code that just ran, which makes it a self-report.

 02

What it tells you is checked against what happened.

Ask for a record to be updated and the natural closing sentence is that it has been updated, whether or not it was.

 03

When it cannot, it says so, naming what’s missing.

The cheapest way to look helpful when a request does not fit is to do the nearest thing that does.

 04

Wrong calls become tests it never fails again.

No single score says whether an assistant’s judgment is improving, only whether it gets a particular kind of call right more often than it used to.
CONNECT

The parts a CIO asks about first

01

From something happening to something finished, one path.

Every other page in this section describes one step.

 02

It acts only as the person who signed in.

Every action carries who is asking and which company they belong to, and both are read from the sign-in, never from the AI (the model).

 03

The alert and your question, one thread that remembers.

Most assistants have two personalities: a chat window that forgets last time, and a notification with no idea what you already said.

 04

The twelfth system costs what the second one cost.

Integration platforms are pleasant in year one and unmaintainable in year three, because somewhere a list has one row per connected system and a person has to remember it.
Honest limits

What this is not.

It is not a search box, because finding the document was never the hard part of the work. It is not a chatbot bolted onto one application, and not a rules engine with a branch per scenario. It is not a system you migrate into, because your data stays exactly where it is today.
