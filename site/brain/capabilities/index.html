The Brain / Capabilities

The Brain, capability inventory

Every capability, in plain language.

The question that matters is not how many apps a system connects to. It is how deep into the work it can go once it is connected. This is the full inventory of what the Brain, WorkElate Chief, does inside a 200 to 2,000 person company: what it notices, what it remembers, what it judges, what it is allowed to touch, and what it is allowed to say it did. 15 verbs, 287 actions, 71 of them classified destructive and gated on a human yes. Nothing here requires you to move your data anywhere.

Sense

What it notices without being asked.

Most enterprise AI waits to be asked. The expensive failures are the things nobody noticed in time. Sense watches the systems you already run, treats a thread that has gone quiet as an event, and keeps interruptions inside a budget of 40 per organization and 5 per person an hour, changed in plain English.

Recall

One memory where there used to be five.

The answer to almost every real question lives in four systems at once. Recall assembles context before an opinion is formed: the graph of related work, the one client behind four spellings, what was decided last time, and the contract itself, read at decision time rather than summarised once at upload.

Reason

Judgment, not rules.

A rules engine with a branch per scenario rots, because nobody can enumerate next quarter’s situations. The Brain gathers the context, weighs it against what this role cares about, asks when an instruction is ambiguous, and says plainly what needs a person. Most of the time the right answer is silence.

Act

15 verbs, 287 actions, 71 of them gated on a human yes.

The Brain has hands, and there are not hundreds of them. 15 verbs, find, read, research, recall, remember, ask, create, edit, improve, delete, send, share, schedule, assign and judge, resolve to 287 actions across the Brain’s own 12 application surfaces and 190 business objects. 71 stop for a human yes, and the verb count only falls.

Prove

What it is allowed to claim.

If a copilot disappointed you before, this is where it happened: it told you something was done. Here a write is re-read before it is believed, the reply is checked against what the tools actually returned, a request that cannot be served is refused by name, and judgment is measured case by case.

Connect and govern

The parts a CIO asks about first.

Two questions decide whether this is a project or a programme: how much of your estate must change, and who can see what. None, and only the people who already could. Identity comes from the session, never from the model, a twelfth system costs what the second did, and your memory is never training material.

One memory where there used to be five

01

A question about one client should read one client's folder and stop.

 02

Ask about the client and get the client, not ten documents that mention them.

 03

 04

 06

01

 02

 03

 06

 03

 04

 05

One request carried across surfaces as one piece of work, each write verified before the next leans on it, stopping only in front of the step with consequences.

 06

Fields, dates and statuses updated once in the system that owns them, then re-read before the update is believed and checked against what the tools returned.

 07

The draft is the unit of work, and it arrives with the context already in it.

Replies, notes and documents composed from the work around them and held unsent, because send is one of the 71 actions that stop for a human yes.
PROVE

What it is allowed to claim

01

A system that never says no cannot be trusted when it says yes.

A request that resolves to nothing returns a named refusal rather than the nearest half-fitting action, and an ambiguous argument is asked about rather than guessed.

 02

A write that reports success is not believed on its word.

A write that reports success is not believed on its word.

 03

Judgment is measured case by case, or it is not measured at all.

Every wrong call becomes a stored case, the suite runs offline against the decision layer, and a class of cases exists purely to catch the system overstating what it did.

 04

The sentence is measured against the data, not against what you asked for.

The reply is checked against what the tools actually returned, never against what you asked for, and a mismatch gets one grounded re-draft or a plain correction.

It is not a system you migrate into, because your data stays exactly where it is today.
