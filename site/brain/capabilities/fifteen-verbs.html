The Brain / Capabilities / The fifteen verbs

ACT

Fifteen verbs reach the whole suite,
and the number is only allowed to fall.

find, read, research, recall, remember, ask, create, edit, improve, delete, send, share, schedule, assign, judge. That is the model’s entire vocabulary. It resolves to 287 actions across the Brain’s own 12 application surfaces and 190 business objects, and adding an application never adds a verb.

What changes on Monday: nothing about how anybody works. New capability arrives as a declaration from the application that owns it, not as a new button and a session in a training room.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

A new surface goes live, and nobody is retrained

10:00

The application declares itself

It states the objects it owns, the addresses its verbs resolve to and the events it emits. That is app-side work, and the right place for it.

11:15

The new objects answer to the sentences people already use

Somebody asks the same kind of question as last week about a kind of thing that did not exist on Friday, and it resolves. There was no command to learn.

11:16 · a person confirms

The first destructive action on the new surface stops

Delete and send are gated by being those verbs, so a brand new action of that shape arrives gated. The first one waits for a click, on day one.

How it works

Three properties a CIO should care about

01

The count only falls

A build gate holds the verb count at 15 and permits it to fall, never to rise. That is a constraint on us, not a design intention in a document.

02

Capability is declared by the application, never taught to the Brain

A surface announces what it is and what it emits; the judgment layer is shared. That is the difference between onboarding a system and rewriting an assistant.

03

A small surface is a reviewable surface

A security review of 15 verbs and one gate is a review a person can finish. 71 destructive actions can be listed and argued about, one by one.

What it is not

Three honest limits

15 verbs is not 15 things it can do. It is 287 actions reached through 15 names, and the second number is the one a buyer should read.

The 12 surfaces are the Brain’s own applications, not your estate. What connects to the systems you already run is settled in an engagement.

A small vocabulary does not make a new surface free. The application still owes its declaration, its objects and its events, in your nouns.
