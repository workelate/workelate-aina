The Brain / Capabilities / Entity resolution

RECALL

Four systems spell your client four ways.
It is still one client.

Northbeam Ltd in finance, Northbeam Limited on the contract, NORTHBEAM in the ticket queue, northbeam-2019 as a folder. Entity resolution decides those are one client, once, and every capability above it stands on that decision. Across 190 business objects, it is the least glamorous work we do.

What changes on Monday: an identity decision a person used to make in their head, repeatedly, is written down and carried everywhere.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

One invoice, three candidates, one question asked

11:02

An invoice arrives carrying a name and nothing else

Crosses the finance system. The billing name reads NORTHBEAM GROUP PLC. No account id, no project code, because the person who raised it typed what was in front of them.

11:02

Three candidates, with different kinds of evidence

Crosses documents, mail and the contract store. One shares an address and a contract reference. One shares a domain on two threads. A dormant folder shares only the word.

11:05

Two are joined, the third is left alone and said out loud

Crosses finance and memory. The invoice attaches to the live client. The dormant folder is not folded in, the Brain says so, and when the finance lead confirms, the answer is remembered for next month.

How it works

Evidence is ranked, and the ranking prefers to stop

01

Identifiers beat structure, and structure beats names

A shared account id or contract reference is strongest. A shared domain, address or existing edge is next. A name alone is a candidate, never a fact, because names collide and get retyped.

02

A wrong join is worse than a missing one

A missing join costs a thinner answer. A wrong join puts one subsidiary’s numbers inside another’s. So the threshold sits where the system stops and asks, not where it looks impressive.

03

The ask is the mechanism, not the fallback

Ask is one of the 15 verbs, alongside find, recall and remember. An ambiguous identity has somewhere to go, and every resolved identity carries its organization, so two organizations cannot resolve into each other.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is never perfect. A complete join across four sources describes a demo, not a deployment.

It does not merge on a strong hunch. Thin evidence stays split, and the Brain says which two things it did not join.

It cannot repair a source with no stable identifier. Some joins will always need a person to settle.
