The Brain / Capabilities / Honest refusal

PROVE

A system that never says no
cannot be trusted when it says yes.

The cheapest way to look helpful when a request does not fit is to do the nearest thing that does. Here a request either resolves to an address under one of 15 verbs or it does not, and there is no third state where the closest match is quietly chosen.

What changes on Monday: when someone asks for something the Brain cannot do on your estate, they get a sentence naming what is missing, that same minute.

What it looks like when it fires

A request for something we do not have yet

01

The request is understood, and then fails to resolve

A finance lead asks for the last four payment runs from a system nobody has connected. The intent is clear. The address is not there, and the failure happens before anything runs.

02

The nearest half-fitting action is not taken

A document search would have returned four spreadsheets with payment in the name and looked like an answer. It is not offered as one, because a confident wrong answer ends the search.

03

The refusal names what is missing

The reply says the system is not connected, what connecting it would need, and what can be answered today from the systems that are. A decision, not a dead end.

How it works

Refusal is a designed path, not a fallback

01

An unresolved address is the answer, not a degraded one

Nothing degrades gracefully into the closest match. Cannot and cannot yet are separate answers, because merging them is how a roadmap turns into a claim.

02

The small vocabulary is what makes the boundary knowable

15 verbs, and a count that only falls, means the edge of the system is a short list rather than folklore. Reach grows as new addresses under existing verbs.

03

Ambiguity is asked about, and known capability is never denied

Where the argument rather than the address is unclear, it asks one question. It may not refuse something it can do, and that behaviour is measured as its own class of stored cases.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a guarantee against confident error. It covers requests that fail to resolve, not every answer that is wrong.

It can be conservative. A capability reachable by an unusual phrasing may be refused once before the phrasing is learned.

Early refusals are the map of what to connect next. Tolerate a no in week one, and name an owner for that list.
