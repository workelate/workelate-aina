The Brain / Capabilities / From signal to finished work

CONNECT

One loop carries a change
from emitted to finished.

Every other page in this section describes one step. This one puts them in order: sense, recall, reason, decide, act or confirm, remember. One path, and a typed question and an emitted event enter at the same place.

What changes on Monday: the handoff that depends on somebody noticing stops depending on it. The change is seen when it happens, and the first person to hear is the one it is for.

What it looks like when it fires

A clause changes on a Wednesday afternoon

01

Sense and recall: the application speaks, the neighbourhood attaches

A contract is edited and the application that owns it declares the event. The work graph returns the client, two dependent tasks and Thursday’s review; memory adds last week’s answer.

02

Reason and decide, and most of the time the answer is silence

The judgment is whether this change is worth interrupting this person now, read against the contract itself. Budgeted at 40 per organization and 5 per person an hour, and this one clears the bar.

03

Act with a confirm, verify, and then remember

A draft note and a re-dated task are proposed, and both stop for a human yes. After the click the write is re-read, the sentence is checked, and the decision goes into memory.

How it works

Where each mechanism sits in the loop

01

Sense is declared by the applications

The Brain holds no list of which applications exist; each declares its own events and addresses, the rule set out on the twelfth application. A system that emits nothing is invisible.

02

Recall is where the intelligence is added, deliberately

Improving the system means improving what context is assembled, never adding branches to the judgment. The asked and the unasked read the same memory, which is one continuous thread.

03

Act is governed by reflexes, and what is said is checked

Identity comes from the session (identity and scope), an unresolvable request is refused by name (honest refusal), and every claim is re-read and checked against the data before it ships.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not autonomy. Anything consequential stops in front of a person, and a declined suggestion is the designed outcome.

It is not a new system of record. Every write ends in the system that owns the object, so there is nothing in our layer to reconcile.

It sees what your systems emit and no more, and it reads your policy from your documents at decision time, so those documents are what we ask for first.
