The Brain / Capabilities / Ranked briefing

REASON

A list without a reason line
is a notification centre.

The briefing is the surface most people meet first, so it decides whether anybody trusts the rest. It is a short ranked list of what needs you, and every item carries two lines no feed has carried: why this is in front of you, and what is holding it up.

What changes on Monday: the first twenty minutes of the week stop being reconstruction. You read a handful of items instead of opening nine tabs, and the first thing you do is decide.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

Four minutes, one list, three decisions.

09:11

Item one is a sign-off, and it says why

Crosses mail and documents. Reason line: due eleven days ago, last message was yours, the version awaiting approval is not the version scoped. Blocker line: the client contact and three changes agreed since.

09:13

Item three is a decision you already made, resurfacing

Crosses chat and the project tool. A date agreed in a channel on Thursday never reached the plan. Nobody is stuck; two systems disagree, and one click reconciles them.

09:14

What is missing from the list is the work it did

Crosses every connected system. Dozens of things moved overnight. Four are on your list. The rest were judged not worth your morning, and that judgment is the product.

How it works

Three mechanisms behind a short list.

01

Candidates come from declared signals, not a crawl

Applications declare their own events, and the briefing reads those as they arrive. A system that emits nothing is invisible to it, and we say so before you find out.

02

No branch ranks overdue above meetings

Ranking is judgment against written policy recalled at decision time, not a priority field. A rules engine with a branch per scenario looks convincing for a quarter, then rots.

03

The list lives inside an interruption budget

At most 40 interruptions per organization per hour and 5 per person per hour by default, with cooldowns so the same item never nags twice. Those numbers change by saying so in plain English.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a feed. If everything that moved reached the list, you would learn to scroll past it in a week.

It cannot raise what nothing recorded.

It does not act on the list for you. Anything consequential waits for a person to click.
