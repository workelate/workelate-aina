The Brain / Capabilities / Move and assign

ACT

A board goes stale because updating it
is always somebody’s least urgent job.

The work moved on Thursday. The card moved on Monday, if anyone got to it. The Brain proposes the move when the evidence for it exists, shows that evidence beside it, and names a blocker rather than painting a cell red.

What changes on Monday: state changes and owners arrive proposed, each carrying the signal that justifies it, and a person spends thirty seconds confirming instead of twenty minutes reconstructing.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

Three moves, one of them refused

09:05

Three cards proposed, each with a line of evidence

One task’s blocking comment was answered in a client reply. One’s dependent document was signed. One has had no activity for eleven days past its due date. Three reasons, three proposed states.

09:06 · a person confirms

She accepts two, and the reason travels with them

The moves run on her click, and the sentence that justified each is written beside the card. In three weeks the reason the date moved is still attached to it.

09:07 · a person declines

The eleven-day card is waiting on a client, not stalled

She declines in one line. The decline is kept, so the next time this pattern appears on the engagement the starting position is what she decided.

How it works

Three mechanisms

01

It sees change because the applications declare it

Every connected application announces what happened in its own words. A move is proposed because something actually occurred, not because a nightly job noticed a date going past.

02

The reason is written where the change is

State, owner and the sentence that justified them go back together into the system that owns the card. No parallel record in our layer, no second place the truth lives.

03

Assignment stops for a person, and so does anything sharp

71 of the 287 actions stop for a human yes, and a yes counts only from a door where a person clicked. A proposed reassignment cannot promote itself.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It does not decide priority for you. It proposes the state that matches the evidence; what matters most this week stays with a person.

It cannot move work in a system that emits nothing. A board only ever updated by hand, in a tool without signals, is invisible to it.

It does not reorganise people. A proposed owner is a suggestion about one task this week, and your states stay in your words.
