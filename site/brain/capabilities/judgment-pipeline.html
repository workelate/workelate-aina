The Brain / Capabilities / The judgment pipeline

REASON

Interrupting a person
is the expensive part.

So there is a path in front of it. Candidates are gathered, mechanical fences are applied, and only what survives is put to the model, once: does one of these deserve this person now, and why. The answer is kept, even when it is no.

What changes on Monday: the thing that reaches you has been through a decision, not a filter, and the far larger number that did not were decided about too.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

From a hundred candidates to one sentence.

11:02

The fences run first, and most candidates stop here

Crosses nothing, deliberately. Budget, cooldown and scope are checked before any thinking is paid for. A hundred form submissions are one item updated, not a hundred nudges.

11:03

One question, one answer, with the reason attached

Crosses the work graph and memory. What survived is put to the model with the surrounding work and the governing document. It names one item and why, or says speaking would not help.

11:03

A withheld item and a silent one are never confused

Crosses memory. A budget stop and a judgment of silence look identical from outside and mean opposite things. They are kept apart, so a quiet system can be told from a broken one.

How it works

Where logic is allowed to live.

01

Mechanical things are mechanical

Budget, cooldown, organization scope and the confirm requirement are reflexes. They never decide a business outcome. 71 of the 287 actions stop for a human confirmation, and a model can never approve its own action.

02

Judgment reads policy as text

What deserves attention is decided against written policy recalled at decision time, not compiled into branches. That is why the same event can produce opposite correct answers for two clients.

03

The reason is kept, not the noise

One decision produces one record of what was chosen and why. A wrong call becomes a stored case, and the stored cases are run against every change to the decision layer.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not a scoring engine with sliders. The fences are configurable; the judgment is not.

It is not free of wrong calls. The cost of one is a decline, which is the trade we designed for.

It does not explain itself at length. The reason is one line, because a paragraph per item is a second inbox.
