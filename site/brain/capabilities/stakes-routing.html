The Brain / Capabilities / Stakes routing

REASON

Labelling a task and wiring a payment
should not cost the same thinking.

How much reasoning a decision gets is decided by what happens if it is wrong. Reversible work runs fast and cheap. Irreversible, client-facing or financial work goes to the strongest reasoning and stops in front of a person. The route is a lookup; the decision never is.

What changes on Monday: the small stuff stops waiting for you, and the consequential stuff stops moving without you.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

Three actions, three different treatments.

08:40

Sorting the overnight mail runs without asking

Crosses mail. Classification is reversible and invisible outside the company. If a label is wrong, somebody changes it and nothing happened. Speed is the right trade.

09:35

A reply to a client is drafted, and stops

Crosses mail and documents. Sending is one of the 71 confirm-gated actions. The draft is ready in seconds; the gap between ready and sent is a person reading it.

11:15

A scope call gets the expensive reasoning

Crosses documents, the project tool and mail. Whether a change is inside the agreement is client-facing and costly to get wrong, so it is read against the actual agreement and presented with the clause attached.

How it works

Three rules, none of them about business outcomes.

01

Route on reversibility and blast radius

Can this be undone, and who sees it if it is wrong. Nothing about the content of the request changes the route, so an urgent-sounding message cannot buy its way past a confirm.

02

The gate is on the action, not the conversation

71 of the 287 actions the 15 verbs resolve to are classified destructive. The classification travels with the action, so the same send is gated from a chat, a briefing or a schedule.

03

A model cannot approve its own action

Consent is minted where a person clicked. An approval appearing in the model’s own output is not consent, because model output is untrusted input.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not an approval workflow. One human yes in front of a consequential write; no delegation matrix, no second signatory.

It does not judge whether an approved action was a good idea. The gate checks that a person decided.

It will not let you switch the confirm off to go faster. That request comes in week three, and the answer is the same.
