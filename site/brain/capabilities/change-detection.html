The Brain / Capabilities / Change detection

SENSE

A notification tells you something moved.
You still have to open the tool to find out what.

Change detection keeps the difference itself: which field moved, on which record, what it was before, who moved it and when. Once the before is held, the question a manager actually asks becomes one read: what is different since I last looked.

What changes on Monday: an alert carries a sentence worth reading, with both values in it, rather than a link and a shrug.

What it looks like on a Tuesday

One field moves, and four other things become false

11:22

An engineer moves a delivery date out two weeks

Crosses the project tool. Stored as the pair: was the fourteenth, now the twenty-eighth, moved by a named engineer with a one-line reason. Nothing has gone wrong yet.

11:22

Four dependents go stale in the same second

Crosses the project tool, documents and the calendar. Two tasks, a paragraph in the statement of work and a client review on the twentieth are now inconsistent with a fact that moved.

11:25

The account lead gets one line, with both dates in it

Crosses documents and mail. Three dependents are internal cleanup for the weekly. The client was told the fourteenth in writing, so that one reaches a person today.

How it works

Three mechanisms

01

The before is captured at the moment of the write

The application emits the change with the prior value attached, in the same transaction. Diffing two snapshots taken hours apart gets you the net result and loses every step.

02

Dependents are derived from the work graph

A task belongs to a project, a document references a commitment, a meeting is about an account. Working out what a change invalidates is a walk over those links, not a rule per field.

03

What a change means is judged, not tabled

There is no severity table. The same date moving is urgent on a contractual milestone and unremarkable on internal work. That call is made against your own written policy, then measured against stored cases.

What it is not

Three honest limits

It is not version control for your business. It holds the difference and a pointer back; the full history stays in the tool that owns it.

It does not know why somebody changed something unless a person wrote the reason down. It will not invent one.

It cannot see a change in a system that was never connected, or one made directly in a database behind the application.
