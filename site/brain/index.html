One client, one Monday

The same five systems. One picture.

A project tool, chat, mail, documents and a calendar. Each holds a piece of the answer, none the answer.

  1. Monday 09:10

    The client arrives as one thing

    Brief, deck, open tasks, thread and the next two meetings, because one folder holds this client.

  2. Monday 09:11

    Blocked items carry the reason

    Two tasks are blocked, each naming why: five comments waiting on the client.

  3. Monday 09:13

    Nine days of silence is a change

    Silence is judged like a date moving: against the role, the work around it, and whether to interrupt.

  4. Monday 09:14

    The chase is drafted in its thread

    A reply names the five open comments, the sign-off and the two dates that move if it slips, unsent.

  5. Monday 09:16 · a person confirms

    The lead edits one line and sends

    Sending stops for a person’s yes; the AI cannot approve itself.

  6. Thursday 10:35 · a person confirms

    The room’s decisions are written back

    Two dates move, one task is reassigned, a follow-up booked; each shown before it runs.
