The Brain / Objections

The eight that kill deals

The objections,
answered rather than handled.

If you are evaluating this, you have already bought AI once and it did not change a number. None of the answers below is a reframe, and four of the eight end with a version of do not buy this. What changes on Monday is that you can take a specific position into your next internal conversation instead of a brochure.

01 · Credibility

The three that are really about credibility

01

We already pay for a copilot

Keep it. A copilot lives inside one application and answers when spoken to. It cannot notice a change nobody asked about, judge whether it is worth interrupting someone, or carry work across systems. If your problem is that drafting takes too long, do not buy this.

02

Our estate is not one vendor, it is fifteen years of decisions

That is the normal case, and the reason this is installed on top rather than sold as a replacement. Your systems stay the systems of record. The limit: what a system emits is what the Brain can see, and the Sprint hands you the list of systems that emit nothing usable.

03

We tried an AI pilot last year and it quietly died

Most do, because they answered questions nobody was blocked on, nobody owned them, or they never touched anything. The first workflow here must cross two systems and have a number somebody already tracks. If none exists, the Sprint should end in a no.

02 · Control

The two that come from security, and should

04

We cannot let anything write to our systems

Then start where nothing does. The Sprint connects one system read only. When a write path opens later, 71 of the 287 actions stop for a person from day one, and nothing can approve itself. If no system may ever write to your estate under any supervision, this is not for you.

05

Our data cannot leave our control

What we can show is mechanism: memory scoped to your organization, identity from the verified session and never from the model. What we cannot show is a certificate, because we hold none. If one is required before a pilot, we fail that gate today.

03 · Capacity and commercial

The three about who carries it, and who pays first

06

We do not have the internal team to run this

Almost nobody your size does. Nothing new becomes a system of record and there is no console to staff. What does need an owner is the written policy the judgment reads; if nobody will own that, do not start.

07

What happens when you disappear

You keep the code, the data and the system, documented, and the right to fire us and take all of it. That term has been on every build since 2018, across 34 products and 9 sectors.

08

Prove it before we pay you anything

We will not work for free, and we make the first paid step small and reversible. The Sprint is two weeks at a fixed fee on your own signals. If it says a build will not move your number, we say so and you stop.

04 · The honest no

When you should not buy this

DO NOT BUY

Your problem lives inside one application

If the pain is drafting or searching within one tool, the copilot you already pay for is the right instrument.

DO NOT BUY

A certification is a hard gate for you

We hold none. If a certificate is required before a pilot can begin, we cannot pass that gate today.

DO NOT BUY

Nobody will own the written policy

Judgment here reads your documents at decision time. If no one will own and correct them, it will drift.

DO NOT BUY

You want an integration platform

If you need two hundred reliable pipes on a schedule, buy one. That comparison does not end in our favour.
