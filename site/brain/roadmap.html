The Brain, roadmap

What we are building next.

Three statuses, and they mean exactly what they say. Shipped means it runs today on a real stack and you can ask to see it. Building now means it is in flight and not finished. Next means we intend to build it and have not started. Five shipped, four building, five next. There are no quarters or months on this page, on purpose: the order is decided by what a paying client needs in production.
