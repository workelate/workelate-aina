The Brain / Getting started

An operator’s checklist

Ten weeks, written out
the way it actually goes.

What changes on Monday is small and specific: two people from your side spend about three hours in week one, one read only credential is issued, and nothing else in your operation moves. Two weeks later you are holding a document that says what the Brain would have caught last week, what it would have stayed quiet about, and what a build costs. This page is that plan, including the parts that are your work rather than ours.

01 · The room

Who needs to be in the room, and for how long

ESSENTIAL · 3 HOURS

The person whose hours are leaking

A function head who names the handoff that hurts and judges the replay at the end.

ESSENTIAL · 1 HOUR

Somebody who can issue a read credential

An administrator for the first system, who can say what it does and does not emit.

RECOMMENDED · 1 HOUR

Your security lead, early

One call in parallel with week one, because the Sprint opens no write path. Send them the security page first.

OPTIONAL

One person who does the work every day

They can say an item the Brain surfaced would have been noise.

02 · Weeks 1 to 2

The Diagnostic Sprint, day by day

Two weeks at a fixed fee, quoted before it starts. The output is a build plan with a price on it.

Days 1 to 2

One system, connected read only

One read credential, so the blast radius of the Sprint is what that credential can reach. The first system is usually the one in the middle of the handoff that leaks.

Days 3 to 5

A real week of your own signals, replayed

A week that already happened, run as if it were live. You watch your own last week, which is the only version that settles an argument.

Days 6 to 8

What it caught, what it stayed quiet about, what it missed

Three lists. The third tells you which of your systems emit nothing useful today.

Days 9 to 10

One workflow chosen, and a build plan with a price

It must cross two systems, have a person carrying things between them, and have a number somebody already tracks.

03 · Weeks 3 to 10

The eight weeks after

One workflow, one team, fixed price, $30K–$100K, quoted before work starts. Nobody changes tools, so the downside of being wrong is one team’s eight weeks.

Weeks 3 to 5

Build, against the plan you already priced

The connections, the write path with the gate in front of it, and judgment calibrated against your written policy. Scope creep is our problem.

Weeks 6 to 7

Shadow running, with a person in front of every write

On day one it proposes more than it should and the team declines a good share. That is the design working.

Weeks 8 to 10

Live, then handover

The team uses it daily. The code, the data and the system are yours, documented, the same term as every build since 2018.

04 · Your side

What we need from you

WEEK ONE

A read credential for one system

Scoped to what the replay needs, revocable at any point.

WEEK ONE

The handoff that leaks the most hours, named

One sentence from the person who owns it.

WEEK ONE

Whatever written policy already exists

Contracts, statements of work, the old escalation note. The Brain judges against these.

THROUGHOUT

A number somebody already tracks

Cycle time, touches per invoice, days to first response.
