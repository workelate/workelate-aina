01 · The part nobody else can copy

Most studios sell you agents.
We hand you the ones running our own company.

WE_AINA is WorkElate's AI-Native Agency. WorkElate is the AI-native work platform we build and sell: mail, calendar, tasks, forms, boards, docs, data, presentations, a hub, an authentication service, an admin console and a public SDK. Our own delivery runs on it. Every engagement in this studio is scoped, built and run inside the product we would sell you.

That is not a philosophy, it is exposure. If the agents cannot carry real work at commercial stakes, we are the first to lose money on it, and we lose it before you do. A studio that only sells the platform finds out from a support ticket. We find out from our own week.

06 · How the engagement works

Three phases. Fixed scope. Receipts at every step.

Weeks 1–2

Diagnostic Sprint

We sit inside your operation, map where hours and money leak, and hand you a build plan with numbers on it, including the fixed price of the build. If we find nothing worth building, we say so in writing.

Weeks 3–10

Build

One system, shipped against the plan at the fixed price from the Sprint, typically $30K–$100K depending on scope. Weekly demos on your real data, not slides. You own the code and the data, no lock-in clause buried on page 14.

Ongoing

Run

The system runs and we stay on it. The monthly report shows hours returned and actions taken, every number tied to work the system actually did.
