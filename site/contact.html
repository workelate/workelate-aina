What happens after you write

Three steps, and you can stop after the first.

Step 1

A reply from Chitransh or Pratik

Not a scheduling link from a coordinator and not a capability deck. One of the two partners reads what you sent and answers it. If what you need is not something we should build, we say that here rather than three meetings later.

Step 2

The Diagnostic Sprint: two weeks, fixed fee

We sit inside your operation, map where hours and money leak, and hand you a build plan with conservative numbers and a fixed price. If the Sprint finds nothing worth building, we tell you in writing. That written no is a real outcome of this step, not a formality, and you keep the plan either way.

Step 3

The build, at the price the Sprint put on it

One system, shipped against the plan at the Sprint price, weekly demos on your real data. Scope creep is our problem, not yours. You own the code and the data; documented handover, and you can fire us and keep everything.
