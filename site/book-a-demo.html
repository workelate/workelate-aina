What the call is, and what you need for it

What to bring

Who you meet: Chitransh and Pratik, the two partners who scope the Sprint and build the system.

No theatre

Three rules we hold ourselves to on the call

01

We demo on your kind of work, not a sample dataset

A scripted dataset makes every product look brilliant. We would rather run your messy week and have the Brain stay silent on something you cared about.

02

We show it staying quiet

A brain that nudges you about everything is the failure mode. On the call we show what got filtered out and why.

03

If it is not a fit we say so on the call

Some operations do not leak enough hours in one workflow to justify a build. We say so in the 45 minutes rather than selling you a Sprint to discover it.

FAQ

What people ask before they book

Do we need to connect anything before the call?

No. To see it on your own signals we connect one system read-only, never with a write credential. Otherwise we use a stand-in set of signals shaped like your work.

How long does it take?

Forty five minutes. Thirty are the Brain reading a week of signals and you reacting. The rest is the list of what it could not do, and your questions.

Who should be in the room?

One person who owns the workflow, plus whoever can say yes to a two week Diagnostic Sprint. An architect or an IT lead is welcome.

What happens to what you see?

A connected system is read-only, so nothing in it changes. Memory is scoped to your organization and not used to train third-party models. If you do not go ahead, we disconnect and the working data goes with it.

What does the demo cost?

Nothing. The Diagnostic Sprint that follows is two weeks at a fixed fee, and builds are fixed price, $30K–$100K, quoted before work starts.

What happens after the call?

A short written note: what the Brain surfaced, what it stayed quiet about, what it could not do, and whether a Diagnostic Sprint is worth your money. If it is not, the note says so.
