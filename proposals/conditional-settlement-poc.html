The idea · How it works

Payments are solved.
Trust is not.

The money rail already exists. The gap is settlement conditioned on delivery: the customer's money sits with a holding partner until the product is confirmed received, then releases to the merchant automatically, or refunds on a return.

Our platform is the authorised API caller that orchestrates that: it creates the payment request through the gateway, tracks the order and delivery, and instructs release or refund to the holding partner.

UPI collection: GPay / Razorpay Funds held by holding partner Release on OTP-verified delivery Refund on return
