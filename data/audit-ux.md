# UX audit, aina.workelate.com, against the Apple bar

Measured 2026-09-15 on http://localhost:4310, Chrome, window 1512 wide (OS minimum; every `clamp()` in site.css resolves to its 1440 maximum at both widths, so the desktop numbers are exact). Mobile measured in a same-origin 390 x 800 iframe. Eighteen pages walked top to bottom, three mega-menu panels opened, nav tabbed by keyboard, work graph watched for ten seconds, FAQ opened and closed, both forms typed into and not submitted. Nothing on the site was changed.

The bar: one idea per screen, the visual carries the idea and the words caption it, generous rhythm, a fixed reading measure, no competing calls to action, progressive disclosure, motion that reveals, a nav that never makes you think.

## Verdict

The site is on brand and nothing is broken, and that is the problem: it reads as sixty well-argued essays wearing the product's dark theme, not as a product. Every first screen is a five-line 64 px headline, a 60 to 170 word paragraph and three calls to action, with the visual, where one exists, pushed below the fold. On /brain the only thing that shows what the product is, the live work graph, starts at y = 876 px at 1440 and is never seen by a visitor who does not scroll; on a phone the same page shows zero calls to action on its first screen. All eighteen pages carry the same primary button to the same place, the studio's contact form, so the product site has no next step of its own and the two zones bleed into one another. Cards that cannot be clicked lift and glow on hover, six accent colours cycle through every grid, and body copy runs 125 characters wide. On a phone the /brain menu is 43 links in a 3,648 px sheet with the primary button five screens down, and the comparison table is 862 px wide in a 350 px box at 12.5 px type. The words are better than most; the screens are not doing the work. A stranger can usually say what a page is about, but only after reading, never after looking, and Apple's whole trick is that looking is enough.

## What passes

Keyboard focus rings, the mega-menu hover intent and Escape handling, form controls at 48 px with visible labels, the honeypot, the 390 px layouts never overflowing, the dark ground and Work Sans on every page, and the honesty of the copy (limits stated, no fabricated quotes). These are table stakes and they are met.

## What was not done

No page was changed. No form was submitted. The hamburger glyph anomaly (#26) was seen once and is reported as unverified. Contrast of accent text on the dark ground was not re-measured here (the design-system note already flags `#6b7280`). The remaining forty-two /brain pages not in the walk list were not opened; the template findings (#2, #3, #20, #23) will apply to them because the chrome is generated.
