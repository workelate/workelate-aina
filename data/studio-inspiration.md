# Studio inspiration: how the best studios use real imagery

Date: 2026-09-28

Sites studied (home plus one case study each; read from fetched page markup, not screenshots):

- Metalab: https://www.metalab.com/ , https://www.metalab.com/work , case https://www.metalab.com/work/uber
- Work & Co: https://work.co/ , https://work.co/clients/ , case https://work.co/clients/ikea/
- Instrument: https://www.instrument.com/ , https://www.instrument.com/work , case https://www.instrument.com/work/oura-smart-ring
- BASIC/DEPT: https://www.basicagency.com/ , case https://www.basicagency.com/case-studies/patagonia-ecommerce-website
- Linear: https://linear.app/ , story https://linear.app/customers/ramp

## 10 takeaways

1. **One wide hero per case, around 16:9 and huge.** Metalab ships case heroes at 2500x1500 and 2500x1455 (Uber), BASIC/DEPT opens Patagonia on a 1920x1080 full-width image. Nothing smaller than the viewport width opens a case.
2. **The case card is a fixed four-part anatomy.** Metalab work cards carry image, client logo, one-line description and service tags ("Product Design, Brand, Engineering"). Instrument cards carry client name, a one-line tagline ("The evolution of EA: from gaming legacy to entertainment leader") and hashtags (#brand #product #marketing). No card has a paragraph.
3. **Outcome first, numbers as the proof.** Work & Co's client list pairs each name with one outcome line and a hard metric ("298% increase in average viewing time"); the IKEA case leads its body with 4x e-commerce growth, 60% online sales lift, 4.8 stars up from 2.5. BASIC/DEPT Patagonia shows exactly two results (25% mobile revenue, #6 in the L2 Digital IQ index).
4. **Product in context beats raw screens for the opener.** Work & Co opens IKEA on a living-room photograph (grey sofa, plywood tables), not a UI; BASIC/DEPT's news grid uses photographs of people, retail and product instead of screenshots. Raw UI only appears lower down, once the story is set.
5. **Rhythm alternates full-bleed and phone-sized strips.** BASIC/DEPT Patagonia runs full-width 1760x990 landscapes, then a row of three or four portrait phone screens (~530x1128), then full width again. Instrument Oura does the same: a 908x1050 animated GIF beside a 908x1241 static phone, then a 1920x992 full-width strip of screens.
6. **Two aspect ratios per grid, not five.** BASIC/DEPT's home uses ~16:10 for news thumbnails (870x544) and a portrait ~4:5 for its internal ventures (630x788, 1144x1430). Instrument's homepage carousel uses square 2048x2048 frames. Each grid picks a ratio and holds it.
7. **Motion is used as a hero, sparingly.** BASIC/DEPT's home hero is a full-bleed video reel; Linear's hero is animated product UI; Instrument Oura puts a single GIF where a still would feel dead. Everywhere else the pages are stills.
8. **Captions are almost absent; section headings do the work.** Instrument and BASIC/DEPT show imagery with minimal or no captions, and let short headed sections ("Storytelling that converts", "Progressive disclosure") explain what the images show.
9. **Metadata sits in a small facts block, not in prose.** Metalab Uber lists Project Type, Stage, Leader and Deliverables as labelled fields under the hero; Linear's Ramp story has a sidebar with founded, switched date and company size (1,600 employees). The reader scans facts in two seconds.
10. **Close each case with people and proof, then the next case.** Instrument Oura ends with three KPI callouts, a client quote with a small 64px headshot, then award badges; Linear's customer stories end with previous and next customer links carrying logos, so the reader never hits a dead end.

## Apply to WE_AINA

- Give every industry and case one 16:9 full-bleed photograph of the work in its real setting (quarry face, truck yard, factory line) before any screen appears; keep product screens for the second beat.
- Standardise the case card to four parts: photo (single 3:2 ratio across the grid), client or sector, one outcome line with a number, two or three tags. No paragraphs on cards.
- Under each case hero, add a four-field facts block (sector, scope, stack, timeline) and two headline metrics, then alternate full-width photo with a row of phone or screen frames.
- Reserve motion for one hero loop at most; everything else stays still on white or #f5f5f7, with teal #0f8083 only on tags and links.
- End each case with a client line and a next-case link so the page flows into the next story.
